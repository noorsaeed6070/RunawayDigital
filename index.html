About Runway Digital

Runway Digital is an aviation project for plane spotters and enthusiasts to record, organise and look back on their aircraft sightings.

This is an early prototype. Sightings are stored in this browser on this device, not in an online account. Clearing this browser's site data may erase your logbook. Do not use this version for sensitive information.
